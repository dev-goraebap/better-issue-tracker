"Solar Icons Set" by 480 Design
(https://www.figma.com/community/file/1166831539721848736), taken from the @iconify-json/solar package
(https://github.com/iconify/icon-sets).

Used as the app's interface icons, in their Linear style (src/shared/ui/solar-icons.ts, made by
scripts/generate-icons.mjs). Only the icons the app names are taken, and their drawings are unchanged; the app draws
them at its own sizes and colours.
